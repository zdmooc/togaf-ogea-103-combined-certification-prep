# Exam Format and Policies — OGEA-103 Preparation

Verified on **2026-09-06** against The Open Group Help Center.

## Part 1 — OGEA-101 Foundation

Official structure:

- **40 multiple-choice questions**
- **60 minutes**
- **60% pass mark**
- topic distribution:
  - Concepts: 8
  - Introduction to the ADM: 14
  - Introduction to the ADM Techniques: 6
  - Introduction to Applying the ADM: 4
  - Introduction to Architecture Governance: 3
  - Architecture Content: 5

Repository consequence: the 300-question bank and all Combined mocks preserve this distribution proportionally or exactly.

## Part 2 — OGEA-102 Practitioner

Official structure:

- **8 scenario-based multiple-choice questions**
- **90 minutes**
- **60% pass mark**
- one answer selected per scenario
- gradient scoring:
  - best: 5
  - second-best: 3
  - third-best: 1
  - incorrect: 0

Official major syllabus areas:

1. Context for Enterprise Architecture
2. Stakeholder Management
3. Phase A: The Starting Point
4. Architecture Development — Phases B, C, D
5. Implementing the Architecture — Phases E, F, G
6. Architecture Change Management — Phase H
7. Requirements Management
8. Supporting the ADM Work

## Open book

Part 2 and Section 2 of the Combined exam provide the relevant Body of Knowledge through the exam `REFERENCE` function.

The current official reference is split into two PDFs:

1. **TOGAF Fundamental Content**
2. **Applicable TOGAF Series Guides**

The correct preparation strategy is therefore not to memorize every paragraph, but to understand the concepts deeply and know how to locate supporting material quickly.

## Combined simulation used in this repository

The repository simulates OGEA-103 as:

- Part 1: 40 questions / 60 minutes
- Part 2: 8 scenarios / 90 minutes
- total training session: **48 items / 150 minutes**, i.e. the sum of the two official part durations

Both sections are scored independently in the mock exams. A strong score in one section does not compensate for a failed training score in the other.

## Retakes

Current official policy for Pearson-delivered exams:

- retakes are allowed after failure;
- there is a mandatory **one-month waiting period** before retaking an exam within the same certification program;
- if a Combined exam has one failed section, the candidate later retakes only the corresponding standalone part after the waiting period.

## ESL time allowance

The Open Group currently documents:

- +30 minutes for eligible Part 1, Part 2 or Bridge exams;
- +30 minutes per part for eligible Combined exams, i.e. +60 minutes total;
- `OGEA-103-ESL` for the Combined ESL exam where automatic ESL registration applies;
- candidates outside automatic ESL eligibility may request approval case-by-case with supporting information/documentation.

Do not assume eligibility from this repository. Check the official ESL page before booking.

## Final booking rule

Always verify the exam duration, language, ESL status and delivery details shown in the Pearson booking confirmation. Public rules and interfaces can change after this repository was finalized.
