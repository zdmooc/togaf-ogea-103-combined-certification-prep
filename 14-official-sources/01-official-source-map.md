## Fundamental Content represented in the Part 2 open book

The current official open-book page lists the following Fundamental Content areas:

- Introduction and Core Concepts
- Architecture Development Method
- ADM Techniques
- Applying the ADM
- Architecture Content
- Enterprise Architecture Capability and Governance

The same page lists applicable Series Guides including guidance on EA capability, practitioner use of the ADM, enterprise agility, business models/capabilities/scenarios, information and organization mapping, value streams, risk/security and digital enterprise.

## Rule for this repository

The repository paraphrases and teaches these concepts. It must not be treated as a substitute for the copyrighted official publications or as a source of official exam items.
