## Source priority

When this repository and an official source differ, use this order of authority:

1. **The Open Group official syllabus / Body of Knowledge**
2. **The Open Group Help Center exam-plan and policy pages**
3. **TOGAF Standard: Fundamental Content and applicable TOGAF Series Guides**
4. this repository's educational explanations, questions, scenarios, mocks and cheat sheets

This repository is a self-study aid. It is not an Accredited Training Course and does not replace the official TOGAF Body of Knowledge.

## Current certification baseline

- Certification syllabus for the TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition: **X2202**.
- OGEA-101 Part 1: **40 multiple-choice questions**, **60 minutes**, **60% pass mark**.
- OGEA-102 Part 2: **8 scenario-based multiple-choice questions**, **90 minutes**, **60% pass mark**, gradient scoring **5/3/1/0**.
- Part 2 is **open book** through the exam `REFERENCE` feature.
- The Part 2 reference consists of two PDFs: **TOGAF Fundamental Content** and **Applicable TOGAF Series Guides**.
