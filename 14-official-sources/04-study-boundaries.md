# Study Boundaries, Copyright and Quality Rules

## 1. Original educational content

The explanations, MayaBank case, 300 Foundation questions, 60 Practitioner scenarios and 6 Combined mock exams in this repository are original educational material created for self-study.

They are not copied official exam questions, leaked items or brain dumps.

## 2. Official TOGAF material remains authoritative

TOGAF is a registered trademark of The Open Group. The official standard, syllabus, study guides and Series Guides remain copyrighted works of The Open Group and their authorized publishers.

This repository paraphrases concepts for learning. It does not reproduce the full standard or substantial copyrighted passages.

## 3. Not an accredited course

This repository is not an Accredited Training Course and does not claim accreditation, endorsement, pass guarantees or psychometric equivalence with the real examination.

## 4. Mock-exam limitations

The mocks reproduce public structural characteristics such as topic distribution, number of questions, scenario format and gradient-scoring logic.

They do **not** prove that:

- item difficulty matches the live exam;
- wording style is identical to the live exam;
- a given mock score predicts a real-exam score exactly;
- all possible TOGAF questions are represented.

Use them as diagnostic tools, not as a certification guarantee.

## 5. Architecture examples

MayaBank, OpenShift, APIs, event streaming, GitOps, observability and similar technologies are examples used to make architecture reasoning concrete.

They are not presented as mandatory TOGAF technologies or official TOGAF solution prescriptions.

## 6. ArchiMate boundary

ArchiMate may be useful professionally for architecture modeling, but it is a separate standard. Any mention of ArchiMate in this repository must be treated as a professional extension, not silently mixed into TOGAF exam requirements.

## 7. Source freshness

The official source map was last verified on **2026-09-06**. Re-check The Open Group before exam registration because exam policies, interfaces, source documents and eligibility rules can change.

## 8. Quality rule for future edits

Any future contribution should preserve these principles:

- official English TOGAF terminology where precision matters;
- clear French explanation around the official term;
- Foundation = knowledge/comprehension;
- Practitioner = application/analysis;
- no copied exam material;
- no synthetic duplicate questions disguised as variants;
- no empty placeholders;
- technical examples clearly separated from TOGAF requirements.
